# Assumptions

All are explicit and most are configurable in the checklist YAML (`assumptions:` block or rubric/rules text). Changing them changes the criterion hash, so affected criteria are re-evaluated.

| # | Topic | Assumption | Rationale | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Criterion 01 “first reply” | The first reply of the **human operator** (`speaker=operator`), even if a bot answered earlier. A bot greeting does not count. Two consecutive operator messages without a client reply in between are treated together. | The checklist evaluates the operator, not the bot. | `assumptions.first_reply: first_human_operator_turn`, rules of 01 |
| 2 | Criterion 07 closing | `closure_mode: strict` (default) = the literal official text «Да — оператор подвёл итог или предложил дальнейшую помощь и попрощался»: farewell **AND** (summary **OR** help offer); a short closing sentence that states the outcome/next step counts as a summary. `closure_mode: lenient` = a polite farewell **OR** a help offer is enough (no summary needed). NA only when «чат оборвался до возможности его завершить». 07 never judges whether the issue was solved — that is 04. | Literal official text by default; lenient is an opt-in for teams that read the PDF more softly (DEV impact in `docs/EVALUATION.md`). | `assumptions.closure_mode` → `{closure_rule}` in rubric/rules of 07 (`cqa/checklist.py: CLOSURE_RULES`); only 07's cache key depends on it |
| 3 | First-response time / explicit wait | The 3-minute first-response threshold, tags and attachments are **not scored**; queue waiting before the operator’s first reply does not lower 06. The same 3 minutes (180 s) is used as the “explicit wait” threshold for later unanswered client messages in the FACTS block. Defined once: `assumptions.long_wait_seconds` in the checklist YAML; the 06 rule text is templated from it (`{long_wait_minutes}`), no constant in code. | Checklist p.2: «порог первой реакции в 3 минуты … в оценку не входят». | `assumptions.long_wait_seconds`, rules of 06 |
| 4 | Calls (organizer note) | Separate checklist `freedom_call_v1`. Criterion 06 assesses logic, clarity, no inappropriate expressions, speech correctness and hold management (warned before hold, thanked for waiting). In all criteria (incl. 04 consultation) ASR recognition errors are ignored and not held against the operator. | Transcripts are noisy ASR. | `checklists/freedom_call_v1.yaml` |
| 5 | Internal hold turns | Operator turns between “consultation initiated / client on hold” and “client no longer on hold” are internal agent talk; marked `[ВНУТРЕННИЕ ПЕРЕГОВОРЫ НА УДЕРЖАНИИ]` and not judged as client communication. | Client cannot hear them. | `assumptions.ignore_internal_hold_turns`, `cqa/data.py` |
| 6 | Audio offset | ISO timestamps → offset = turn time − first turn time; used to seek the audio player. Approximate: the recording may not start exactly at the first transcript turn. | No alignment metadata in input. | `cqa/data.py` |
| 7 | Rounding | `Decimal` ROUND_HALF_UP, 2 decimals. | Avoid float/banker’s rounding surprises (50.625 → 50.63). | `assumptions.rounding/decimals` |
| 8 | Languages | One checklist for RU/KZ/EN; criterion 01 requires greeting in the **client’s** language. | Organizer spec. | rules of 01 |
| 9 | Criterion 02 NA | NA = the request was clear and answerable from the start (organizer rubric). 02 judges only the pre-answer stage; missed/incorrect answers belong to 04/08. A clarification the client never answered (client left) still counts as YES. | Organizer rubric; gold-set tuning showed models mixing answer quality into 02. | rules of 02 |
| 10 | Criterion 04 anchors | 20 all closed; 15 minor detail missed; 10 one essential of several unanswered; 5 only a general hint; 0 wrong key recommendation. A correct concrete next step/escalation counts as closing (20) even without a stated deadline. | Organizer scale, made explicit for multi-question requests. | rules of 04 |
| 11 | Speaker roles | `agent/manager/specialist/оператор…`→operator, `customer/user/клиент…`→client, `assistant/ivr/aim…`→bot. Unknown roles are kept as `UNKNOWN` (never credited to the operator) and reported in the dialog `warnings`. | Robustness to other exports. | `cqa/data.py` |
| 12 | Timestamps | `mm:ss`, `hh:mm:ss`, plain seconds, ISO with/without tz (7-digit fractions trimmed) for both chats and calls; timestamps are part of the dialog hash. | Robustness; cache invalidates if timing changes. | `cqa/data.py` |
| 13 | Evidence | Empty evidence is allowed for NA (needs `na_reason`) and NO-by-omission (needs `missing_where`). Positive verdicts need operator quotes: one retry, then kept with `evidence_warning`. 08=YES without an operator quote → retry, then kept as YES but flagged `needs_review` (never silently flipped). | Avoid fabricated quotes and silent flips. | `cqa/evaluator.py` |
| 14 | Untrusted transcript | Transcript is wrapped in `<<<TRANSCRIPT_START>>>…<<<TRANSCRIPT_END>>>`, delimiter collisions are escaped and the system prompt states that the transcript is data, not instructions. Calls get an ASR caveat. | Prompt-injection safety (see gold-16). | `cqa/prompts.py` |

## Rubric alignment (official checklist PDF vs. our YAML)

Audit of every criterion against the official text (checklist PDF pp. 1–3) and the organizer clarifications for calls
(06 = logic, clarity, no inappropriate expressions, speech correctness, pause management; 04 = answered questions,
no wrong information, explained actions). Rule: where our YAML was stricter or more specific than the official text
and the extra rule is not implied by it, the rule was removed or softened. Rules that only restate or operationalise
the official text were kept. No change was tuned on gold labels.

| Crit. | Official sentence (quoted) | Our rule before | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 06 | «В этом задании порог первой реакции в 3 минуты, теги и вложения в оценку не входят.» | “explicit wait” = reply delayed > **2 min** (constant 120 s in code + YAML + text) | **Changed** to 180 s, single source `assumptions.long_wait_seconds`; first-response time still not scored. |
| 06 | «**7** Один заметный сбой, но клиент может понять ответ и продолжить.» | 7 also for “лишний шаблон” (an extra template phrase) | **Removed** “лишний шаблон”; added: template/polite phrases, brevity or dryness do not lower 06 when the answer is clear (tone is 05). |
| 06 | «**4** Клиенту приходится переспрашивать из-за формулировок или порядка ответов.» | 4 required a re-ask **and** “несколько сбоев”; the example gave 7 for a single re-ask | **Softened** to the official condition: a re-ask caused by wording/order → 4. |
| 06 | «**0** Из-за бессвязных или противоречивых реплик ход диалога непонятен.» | “Противоречивые ответы — 4 или 0” | **Aligned**: 0 only if the flow became unclear; a contradiction the operator then clarified → 4. |
| 06 (calls) | organizer: logic, clarity, no inappropriate expressions, speech correctness, pause management | logic, clarity, inappropriate expressions, pauses (speech correctness missing) | **Added** speech correctness (wording/vocabulary, not ASR distortions); inappropriate expression named as a 7-level glitch. |
| 04 | «**20** Все существенные вопросы закрыты или дан корректный следующий шаг.» | next step counted only “С объяснением, что будет дальше (срок, кто свяжется…)”; escalation without deadline/channel = **10** | **Softened**: a correct concrete next step (escalation/ticket/right department) = 20 even without a deadline; a missing deadline is at most a missed secondary detail (15) if the client asked for it. |
| 04 | organizer: answered questions, no wrong info, explained actions | — | **Added** as the explicit checklist of what 04 looks at. |
| 07 | «Да — оператор подвёл итог или предложил дальнейшую помощь и попрощался.» | “Нужны оба элемента: (резюме или предложение помощи) + прощание”; models read “оба элемента” as summary **and** offer (e.g. rejected «Тағы сұрақтарыңыз болса, жазыңыз» + farewell) | **Clarified** the formula: farewell + **one of** (summary, offer); a short closing sentence that states the outcome counts as a summary. A bare «Хорошего дня!» with neither element stays NO in `closure_mode: strict` (default; literal text and the PDF's own example «закрыл чат без прощания» → Нет); `closure_mode: lenient` accepts farewell OR offer. **Added** «Не оценивай, решён ли вопрос клиента — это критерий 04» (DEV justifications had leaked 04 into 07). |
| 02 | «Да — оператор учёл известные детали и уточнил недостающее до ответа. N/A — данных для ответа хватало с самого начала.» | NA when the request was answerable from the start; NO for re-asking known details | **Kept** (literal: “учёл известные детали” implies not re-asking). The high NA share on DEV (43/65) follows from the official N/A condition on simple synthetic requests. **Added** three short synthetic examples (NA / YES / NO) to the 02 rules to delimit N/A («данных хватало с самого начала») from YES (data was missing and the operator obtained it before answering). |
| 01 | «Да — оператор поздоровался на языке клиента и представился в первом ответе.» | company name without a personal name = NO | **Kept** (“представился” = named themself). |
| 03 | «Да — проверка личности подтверждена до раскрытия данных или защищённого действия.» | self-stated name/IIN without a check + disclosure = NO | **Kept** (official: “подтверждена”, and p.3 «не подтверждена в чате — ставьте Нет»). |
| 05 | «…если клиент выразил недовольство или описал неудобство, отреагировал на конкретную проблему.» | a generic template apology to an explicit complaint = “скорее NO” | **Kept** (“на конкретную проблему”). |
| 08 | «оскорбление клиента, предложение платной услуги в обход компании или раскрытие конфиденциальной информации» + p.3 «Достаточно самого предложения» | same three categories, proposal suffices | **Kept**. |

## Variance reduction and review flags

| Topic | Rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Scale criteria decided by the judge | When jurors disagree (or the judge confirms/decides) on 04/06, the judge is sampled `CQA_JUDGE_SAMPLES` = 3 times and the **median** step is final (lower median on ties; NA only with a strict majority). The blind-judge shortcut is used for binary criteria only. | `Evaluator._judge`, `median_step` |
| Binary criteria | Majority of three: two jurors + the judge breaking a 1:1 tie. | `Evaluator._jury` |
| `needs_review` | Set when jurors disagreed, the judge decided, judge samples split, a scale value has confidence < 0.7, or 08=YES lacks a verified operator quote. Reasons in `review_reasons`. Internal results + UI only; the v1.0 export is unchanged. | `review_reasons` |
| `stability` (per dialog) | Unanimous criteria share and the min–max final score obtainable from the values the models actually voted for. A worst-case spread over model disagreement, not a repeated-run interval. | `dialog_stability` |
| Call metrics | Operator words/min, estimated overlap rate (next speaker started before the turn could be spoken at 2.5 words/s) and operator response pauses, from turn start times only. Informational, not scored. | `data.call_metrics` |
